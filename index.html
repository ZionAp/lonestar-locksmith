Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Austin homeowners & businesses

What locksmith services does Austin Locksmith Pros offer?

We provide 24/7 emergency lockout service for homes, businesses, and vehicles, residential and commercial rekeying, lock repair and replacement, key duplication, car key replacement and programming, smart lock installation, and access control. Every job is handled by a licensed locksmith with upfront pricing.

How fast can you get to me in Austin?

For most addresses in the Austin metro, our average arrival time is 10 to 45 minutes. We are a fully mobile service, so we come to you - no towing, no trip to a shop. Call (737) 265‑4627 and we will give you an honest ETA for your location.

Do I need to show ID before you unlock my home or car?

Yes. Our locksmiths verify proof of residence or ownership before opening any property - a driver's license plus a utility bill, lease, or vehicle registration is usually enough. This protects you and is standard practice for licensed locksmiths.

Are you available on weekends and holidays?

Yes. We are available around the clock, every day of the year, including weekends and holidays. Emergency lockout service, rekeying, and most repairs can be handled on the spot whenever you call.

What lock and key brands do you work with?

We work with Schlage, Kwikset, Medeco, Mul-T-Lock, Baldwin, Yale, Arrow, and most other residential, commercial, and automotive lock brands. If you need a specific brand or high-security cylinder, call and we will confirm what we can source for you.

Which areas around Austin do you serve?

We serve Austin and 21 surrounding communities, including Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Lakeway, Bee Cave, Westlake Hills, Dripping Springs, Buda, Kyle, San Marcos, and more. See our service area guides for city-specific details.
