Which Locks Are Hard to Open?

Certain locks pose significant challenges even for experienced locksmiths. Here are the types that are hardest to open.

  • High-security locks - precision keyways, drill-resistant components, complex pin configurations
  • Electronic and smart locks - proprietary software, encryption, biometric scanners
  • Bank vaults and safes - multi-layered security, time locks, relocking mechanisms
  • Automobile locks - transponder keys, sidebar pins, electronic ignition
  • Specialty and custom locks - unusual mechanisms, limited documentation
